Multi-center intracranial EEG

Patient-relative evidence for epileptogenic zone localization.

EpiLENS combines seizure-consistent patient-relative deviations with complementary boundary-aware ranking, improving balanced localization across heterogeneous patients and clinical centers.

Method

Conservative dual evidence, organized around the patient.

EpiLENS constructs four self-referenced feature views and processes them through two independent branches. CDEL keeps PRQ-Net primary and adds a restrained contribution from BCR-Net at inference.

01

Patient-relative views

Absolute, reference-difference, standardized-deviation, and log-relative features expose within-patient change while preserving the original electrophysiology.

02

Asymmetric branches

PRQ-Net produces stable patient-relative probabilities. BCR-Net focuses supervision on ambiguous boundaries and annotated-set coverage.

03

Locked conservative fusion

CDEL uses 0.8 PRQ-Net + 0.2 BCR-Net. A single validation-selected fold threshold is applied unchanged to outer-test patients.

Results

Balanced localization without inflating the predicted EZ set.

Patient-wise metrics on fixed five-fold splits, reported over three seeds. Macro-F1 is the primary endpoint.

Macro-F1 0.6371 ± 0.0072

+0.0306 over the strongest baseline

EZ-F1 0.4485 ± 0.0118

Worst-center Macro-F1 0.5773 LOCO

Best worst-center result in leave-one-center-out evaluation

The strongest retained baseline is logistic regression with patient-wise z-score normalization (Macro-F1 0.6065). Clinical annotations are surgical-target surrogates, not definitive biological ground truth.

Evidence

What the analyses support.

Complementary ranking

BCR-Net improves ordering of difficult EZ candidates; CDEL retains most of that gain while producing the strongest thresholded localization.

Cross-seizure consistency

Performance rises as repeated seizures are added under frozen checkpoints, fusion weights, and validation thresholds.

Unseen-center transfer

Leave-one-center-out experiments retain the strongest center-average and worst-center Macro-F1 without target-center adaptation.

Citation

Use EpiLENS in your research.

The code implements the patient-relative models, locked CDEL fusion, strongest baseline, and the reported analysis workflows. Clinical data are not distributed.
