Abstract

EpiLENS is a patient-relative framework for localizing the epileptogenic zone from multi-center intracranial EEG. It combines stable patient-relative probabilities from PRQ-Net with complementary boundary-aware ranking evidence from BCR-Net through Conservative Dual-Evidence Localization (CDEL). Evaluated on 80 patients from four centers, EpiLENS reaches a patient Macro-F1 of 0.6371 while maintaining near-zero EZ-fraction bias.
